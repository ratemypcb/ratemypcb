# Phase 2 Usability Protocol

## Participants and cases

Run at least 10 anonymized trials with representative PCB/release engineers across blocked and approved synthetic goldens. Do not store identities or recordings. Record viewport, case, answer, elapsed seconds, and error notes.

## Tasks

1. Without scrolling, state the release disposition and first action within 10 seconds.
2. Explain `not checked`; correct means unavailable/unperformed evidence, not pass or zero risk.
3. Follow one claim ID, identify artifact, producer/version, location, and freshness, copy the exact ID, then return to the claim.

## Acceptance

- Disposition and first-action accuracy: ≥9/10 within 10 seconds.
- Unknown interpretation: ≥9/10 correct.
- Traceability: all trials resolve provenance with zero dead links.
- Report numerator/denominator and every miss; do not substitute averages.
